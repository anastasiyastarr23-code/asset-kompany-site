ORZO Market

Grocery delivery at wholesale prices

A grocery marketplace: goods from suppliers at wholesale prices, delivered across Tashkent.

  • Wholesale prices

    Groceries at wholesale prices, delivered to your door, with no trips to a warehouse.

  • Now or at a set time

    Delivery as soon as possible or at a time you choose, from 07:00 to 23:00. Order status arrives as notifications.

  • Cash or online

    Pay the courier in cash, or online with Click, Payme, Uzum or a bank card.

  • Out of stock? You get it back

    If an item turns out to be out of stock, we remove it from the order and recalculate the total. Online payments for it are refunded automatically.
