These terms are a public offer by ASSET KOMPANY LLC (the “Seller”) for the distance sale of groceries and related goods with delivery through the ORZO Market mobile app (the “App”). By placing an order, you (the “Buyer”) accept these terms.

Account

  1. You need an account to place an order. Accounts are created with a phone number, which is confirmed with a code.
  2. The Buyer provides accurate information and is responsible for actions taken in their account.
  3. A business Buyer also provides its company name, TIN, address and bank details.
  4. The Buyer can delete their account at any time. See Delete your account.

Goods and prices

  1. The description, photos, price and unit of sale are shown on each product page in the App.
  2. Prices are in Uzbek soums and include VAT where the Seller is a VAT payer.
  3. The price is fixed when the order is placed. For goods sold by weight, the final amount is calculated from the actual weight at picking and may differ slightly from the amount shown at checkout.
  4. Product photos are for illustration. Packaging may differ if the manufacturer has changed it.

Minimum order

The minimum order amount and the delivery fee are shown in the App before checkout. The Seller may change them; changes do not apply to orders already placed.

Payment

  1. Orders are paid online in the App or on delivery. The payment methods available for a given order are shown at checkout.
  2. Online payments are processed by payment providers: licensed payment institutions of the Republic of Uzbekistan. The Seller does not receive or store bank card data.
  3. A business Buyer may pay by invoice where this option is available in the App.
  4. If the final amount decreases after picking, the difference is refunded by the same method used to pay.

Delivery and acceptance

  1. The Buyer chooses the delivery address and time slot at checkout. Available slots are shown in the App.
  2. The courier hands the order to the Buyer or to a person at the delivery address.
  3. On receipt, the Buyer checks the quantity, completeness and appearance of the goods, the condition of the packaging and the expiry dates.
  4. Visible defects must be reported to the courier on delivery or to support on the day of delivery.

Expiry dates

The Seller delivers goods with enough shelf life left for their intended use. If a product has expired or expires on the day of delivery, the Buyer may refuse it on delivery.

Substitutions

If a product is out of stock, the Seller offers a substitute. A substitution is made only with the Buyer’s consent, given in the App, by phone or by message. If the Buyer does not agree or does not reply, the product is removed from the order and its price is not charged or is refunded.

Returns and exchanges

  1. Returns and exchanges follow the consumer protection laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
  2. For goods of inadequate quality, the Seller replaces them or refunds their price. Contact support and, if possible, attach a photo of the product and its packaging.
  3. Food products of adequate quality cannot be returned or exchanged unless the law provides otherwise.
  4. Refunds are made by the same method used to pay, within the period set by law.

Liability

  1. The Seller is responsible for the quality of the goods and for meeting delivery times, except where a failure is caused by the Buyer or by force majeure.
  2. The Seller is not responsible for delays if the Buyer gave a wrong address or could not be reached during the chosen time slot.
  3. The Seller is not responsible for temporary unavailability of the App due to maintenance or network operator outages.

Complaints

Send complaints to support@asset-kompany.uz with your order number. The Seller reviews each complaint and replies within the period set by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan. Disputes are resolved by negotiation and, failing that, by a court at the Seller’s location, unless consumer protection law provides otherwise.

Changes to these terms

The Seller may change these terms. The new version is published on this page and applies to orders placed after publication.
