ORZO Market

Grocery delivery at wholesale prices

A mobile app for ordering groceries at wholesale prices with delivery. Both individuals and organisations can place orders.

  • Coming soon to the App Store
  • Coming soon to Google Play

Developer and rights holder of the app: ASSET KOMPANY LLC

Features

  • Wholesale prices

    Groceries at wholesale prices, delivered to your door, with no trips to a warehouse.

  • A delivery slot that suits you

    Choose the address and time at checkout, then follow the order status in notifications.

  • For businesses

    Buy as a company or sole trader, with your details saved in the profile, invoices and accounting documents.

  • Substitutions only with your OK

    If an item is out of stock, we offer a substitute. Nothing is swapped without your consent.
