This policy explains how ASSET KOMPANY LLC processes the personal data of users of the ORZO Market mobile app.

Who processes your data

The data controller is ASSET KOMPANY LLC, taxpayer ID (TIN) 312691234, address: 52-A Quyliq-5 Massif, Xalqobod MFY, Sergeli District, Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

For any question about your data, email support@asset-kompany.uz or call +998 88 644 20 20.

What data we collect

  • Name, to address you and hand your order to the courier.
  • Phone number, to sign you in and contact you about your order.
  • Delivery addresses, to deliver your order.
  • Location, only if you allow it in your phone settings. It helps you pick an address on the map. The app works without it; you can type the address instead.
  • Order history: items, amounts, dates and order statuses.
  • Business details, if you buy as a company or sole trader: company name, TIN, address and bank details for invoices and documents.
  • Device data: device model, operating system version, app version, language and technical error logs.
  • Push token, to send order status notifications. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings.

We do not collect bank card data. Card details are entered and processed by payment providers: licensed payment institutions of the Republic of Uzbekistan. We only receive the result of the payment from them: whether it succeeded, the amount and the transaction number.

Why we use your data

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To accept, pick, deliver and take payment for orders.
  • To contact you about your order and answer support requests.
  • To issue invoices and accounting documents to business customers.
  • For accounting and tax purposes.
  • To find and fix errors and to prevent fraud.
  • To send order status notifications.

We send marketing messages only with your separate consent, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share data with

  • Couriers and delivery services: your name, phone, address and order contents, only to deliver the order.
  • Payment providers: the data needed to process a payment.
  • Our hosting provider: data is stored on its servers; it does not use the data for its own purposes.
  • Government authorities: only where required by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for their advertising.

Where your data is stored

Personal data of citizens of the Republic of Uzbekistan is collected, organised and stored on servers located in the Republic of Uzbekistan, as required by the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data” No. ZRU-547.

How long we keep data

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except for order and payment records. We are required to keep those for accounting and tax purposes for {{period, to be confirmed by the accountant}}.

Your rights

You can:

  • find out what data we process about you;
  • correct inaccurate data in your app profile or through support;
  • withdraw your consent to data processing;
  • delete your account.

To exercise these rights, email support@asset-kompany.uz. We will reply within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app or by emailing support, without installing the app. See Delete your account.

Children

The app is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new “last updated” date. We will notify you in the app about significant changes.
